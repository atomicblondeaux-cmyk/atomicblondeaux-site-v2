03 / About
NDH
Founder & Principal

Nedra Davis Hains, D.Des.

An interdisciplinary practitioner working across infrastructure, cultural preservation, public policy, and emerging technology.

Dr. Hains brings more than two decades of experience with engineering, coastal restoration and protection, hurricane protection, public-sector programs, cultural institutions, historic preservation, and infrastructure advocacy throughout Louisiana and the southeastern United States.

Her Doctor of Design in Cultural Preservation from Louisiana State University builds upon a career spent navigating the spaces between technical systems and the communities, institutions, histories, and places they serve.

She served three terms on the East Baton Rouge Parish Historic Preservation Commission and has remained actively engaged in infrastructure policy and engineering advocacy through ASCE.
